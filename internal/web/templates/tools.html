Weight-Based Dosage Calculator

Arithmetic helper only — always verify age limits, maximum daily doses, and contraindications in Drug Labels or Remedy.

  • Inputs: Patient Weight (body mass in kg/lb), Target Dose (prescribed mg per kg), Doses/Day (daily administrations), Strength (mg per tablet or per mL/5 mL).
  • Outputs: Patient Weight (converted kg/lb), Single Dose (exact mg & tablets or mL per dose), Daily Total (combined 24-hour mg across all doses).

Solar & Battery Runtime Sizing

  • Inputs: Capacity & Voltage (battery size), Usable DoD (safe discharge %: ~80% LiFePO4, ~50% Lead-Acid), Efficiency (inverter/wiring losses), Load & Solar Panel (continuous draw vs panel peak Watts).
  • Outputs: Total Capacity (raw stored Wh), Usable Energy (safe deliverable Wh after losses), Estimated Runtime (how long the battery runs your load), Full Recharge Time (peak sun hours at ~85% controller yield).

Radio Frequency, Wavelength & Antenna Length

  • Inputs: Frequency (operating channel in MHz) and Velocity Factor (0.95 accounts for wire end-effects vs 1.0 in vacuum).
  • Outputs: Full Wavelength (λ distance per wave cycle), Half-Wave Dipole (total wire span & each λ/4 leg from feedpoint), 1/4-Wave Vertical Whip (single element over a ground plane).

Format Converter (WGS84)

Point A → Point B Distance & Bearing

  • Inputs: Point A & Point B entered as lat, lng Decimal Degrees (or loaded from saved Map pins).
  • Outputs: DMS (Degrees-Minutes-Seconds), DDM (Degrees-Decimal-Minutes), WGS84 UTM (grid zone & meters east/north), Great-Circle Distance, Compass Bearing A→B / Return, and walking ETA at 4.5 km/h.

Unit Converter

  • Inputs: Category (measurement family), Value (numeric amount), and From Unit (starting unit).
  • Outputs: Simultaneous conversions across all metric, US customary, and field/nautical units in that category.
